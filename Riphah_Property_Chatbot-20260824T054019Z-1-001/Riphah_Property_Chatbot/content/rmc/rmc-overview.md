# Riphah Medical City — Project Overview

Source: *RMC Brochure* (Riphah Properties), supplied by Riphah. Positioned in the
brochure as **South Asia's first healthcare REIT**. Public project website:
www.RiphahMedicalCity.com

## What it is

Riphah Medical City is a purpose-built healthcare destination located directly on
the Islamabad Expressway, designed to meet growing demand for advanced medical
services, diagnostics and allied healthcare businesses.

The project integrates a **600-bed tertiary care hospital** with the **Care Connect
Tower**, creating a high-footfall, business-ready environment that supports medical
practitioners, healthcare investors and service providers.

It is a healthcare-integrated, mixed-use development bringing medical education,
healthcare services, research facilities, commercial units and supporting
infrastructure into a single master-planned ecosystem. It is developed under
Riphah Properties, the real estate arm of the Riphah institutional ecosystem.

## Key specialties and departments

Described in the brochure as a regional Centre of Excellence for advanced,
technology-driven and multidisciplinary care:

- Stem Cell Therapy Centre
- Neurology & Neurosurgery
- Spinal Surgery
- Gamma Knife (radiosurgery)
- Cochlear Implant Surgery
- Cornea Transplant (Ophthalmology)
- Haematology & Bone Marrow Transplant
- Liver Transplant & Hepatobiliary Surgery
- Kidney Transplant (Urology & Nephrology)

Comprehensive transplant solutions are a stated focus of the project.

## Why Islamabad

Nestled against the Margalla Hills, Islamabad combines modern urban planning,
natural beauty and institutional stability. As Pakistan's capital it attracts
professionals, healthcare institutions, investors and families seeking long-term
security, quality of life and sustainable growth. Its infrastructure, expanding
healthcare demand and regulatory maturity make it a natural home for a
next-generation medical ecosystem.
