# The Riphah Group — Institutional Profile

Source: *RMC Brochure* (Riphah Properties).

Founded in 1995 as the **Islamic International Medical College Trust (IIMCT)**,
the Riphah Group has grown into one of Pakistan's most respected institutional
families, impacting millions across seven sectors.

## Group vision for healthcare

Riphah Group advances Pakistan's growth through transformative education,
innovative development projects via Riphah Properties, and world-class healthcare
at Riphah Medical City. Together these pillars are intended to create a trusted
ecosystem that empowers communities, drives sustainable progress, and sets
standards for excellence, well-being and social impact.

Riphah Medical City envisions becoming a top regional healthcare destination,
combining medical services with research and technology. By integrating REIT
models the Group aims to support sustainable growth, enhance healthcare
infrastructure, and provide accessible care across the region.
