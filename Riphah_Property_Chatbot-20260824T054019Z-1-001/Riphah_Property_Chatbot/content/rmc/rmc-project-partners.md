# Riphah Medical City — Project Partners

Source: *RMC Brochure* (Riphah Properties). The project is developed in
collaboration with globally recognised partners to ensure international standards
of design, engineering and execution.

## REIT management — Arif Habib Dolmen REIT Management Limited (AHDRML)

Pakistan's first REIT management company. A joint venture between the Arif Habib
Group and the Dolmen Group, incorporated in 2009 and registered under the NBFC
Rules with the SECP. It combines Arif Habib's financial expertise with Dolmen's
property development capability, and works in investments, market gap
identification and 360-degree property management.

AHDRML manages 10 REIT schemes across Pakistan and has been recognised as the
Most Outstanding Company in Pakistan's Real Estate Sector at the Asiamoney Awards
for six consecutive years.

## Audit and advisory — A.F. Ferguson & Co. (PwC Pakistan)

Founded in 1893, one of Pakistan's leading firms in public accounting, business
advisory and corporate services — audit, accounting, tax, management consulting
and financial advisory. A member of the global PwC network, operating from
Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad, serving blue-chip companies, financial
institutions and multinationals.

## Medical design consultant — HKS Architects, USA

A globally renowned healthcare design and medical planning firm founded in 1939
and headquartered in Dallas, Texas, with more than 20 offices worldwide. Brings
expertise in healthcare master planning, patient-centric design, and sustainable
future-ready medical facilities. Their involvement is intended to ensure RMC
meets international healthcare planning and operational benchmarks.

## Structural and MEP design — Meinhardt Group

A global engineering consultancy founded in 1955 and headquartered in Singapore,
with a presence in over 50 offices worldwide. Provides integrated structural and
MEP design, safety-driven and efficient engineering solutions, and
sustainability- and energy-optimised systems.

## Construction partner — Izhar Group of Companies

One of Pakistan's most established construction firms, founded in 1959 and
headquartered in Lahore, with a track record across commercial, residential,
industrial and infrastructure projects. Brings proven execution capability,
quality assurance, and delivery aligned with international standards.

## Main sponsor — Riphah Group

Founded in 1995, a leading national institution in education, healthcare and
research. Through Riphah International University and Riphah Healthcare Services
the Group operates multiple teaching hospitals, medical and health sciences
programmes, and research and innovation platforms. It provides the institutional
backbone that anchors Riphah Medical City.
