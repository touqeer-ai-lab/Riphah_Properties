# Riphah Medical City — Development Scope and Phasing

Source: *RMC Brochure* (Riphah Properties) and *RMC – REIT Investor FAQs*.

Classified `reference` deliberately. Construction dates move, and the investor
FAQ itself states that **specific timelines are shared in official development
updates** — so this informs an answer but should not be quoted to a visitor as a
committed date. Point the visitor at the sales team or the official development
update for a current date.

## Phased approach

The brochure describes a phased approach intended to ensure controlled growth,
operational readiness and sustainable long-term performance.

### Phase I — 200 beds

Brochure states completion by **July 2027**.

- Initial operational capacity
- Full suite of core specialties
- Advanced imaging and diagnostics
- Emergency and critical care units

### Phase II — a further 400 beds, reaching full 600-bed capacity

- Super-specialised transplant centres
- Nuclear medicine and radiosurgery
- Academic and research integration

## Why it is phased

Per the investor FAQ, phasing is designed to deliver:

- Capital efficiency
- Demand alignment
- Infrastructure readiness
- Risk mitigation

## Construction progress reporting

The investor FAQ commits to the following updates for investors:

- Monthly construction updates
- Quarterly investor newsletters
- Photo and video documentation
- Milestone announcements
- Bi-annual investor webinars
- Live time-lapse camera access, for transparency and assurance

## Known risk to timelines

The investor FAQ lists **construction timeline delays** among the key risks
investors should consider. Do not present any date as guaranteed.
