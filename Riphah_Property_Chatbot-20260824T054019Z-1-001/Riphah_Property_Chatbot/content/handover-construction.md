# Construction Status and Handover Process

## Why this is `reference` and not `public`

Construction progress and handover dates are the second-most-quoted thing in a
property enquiry after price, and a stated completion date is read by a buyer as a
commitment. The scope document is explicit: the assistant does not state
completion or handover dates unless they exist in published Riphah content
(s8).

Classifying this `reference` lets the assistant explain *how* handover works and
*what* the stages are, while the prompt forbids it from quoting specifics from
here as though they were confirmed dates. A visitor asking "when will Block B be
handed over?" gets the process and a referral, not a date.

## Handover stages

1. **Structural completion** — the block's frame, envelope and core services are
   complete.
2. **Services commissioning** — electrical, plumbing, HVAC, lifts and fire systems
   are tested and signed off.
3. **Authority completion approvals** — the relevant authority's own inspection
   and completion certification. Timelines here are the authority's, not the
   developer's.
4. **Possession notice** — issued to buyers whose accounts are clear, inviting
   them to settle the final balance and take possession.
5. **Snagging window** — a defined period after possession in which the buyer
   lists defects for rectification.
6. **Defect liability period** — the developer's rectification obligation for
   specified defects, running from possession for the period set in the booking
   documentation.

## What buyers should understand about dates

Three things account for most handover disputes, and a consultant should raise
all three at booking rather than at possession:

- **A target date is not a contractual date** unless the booking documentation
  says it is. Ask which one you have been given.
- **Authority approval is outside the developer's control.** Stage 3 above can
  move a handover by months for reasons no one at the development can influence.
- **Possession requires a clear account.** A buyer with an outstanding balance
  does not receive a possession notice, however complete the block is.

## Shell-and-core versus finished

Medical suites and office floor-plates are handed over shell-and-core: structure,
envelope, core services brought to the unit boundary, and nothing else. Fit-out
begins after possession and typically takes a further period the buyer should plan
for — a suite is not usable the week it is handed over.

Apartments are handed over finished, to the specification listed in the booking
documentation.

## Maintenance from possession

Common-area maintenance charges begin at possession, whether or not the buyer
occupies or fits out the unit. This surprises buyers who intend to leave a suite
empty until they find a tenant, so it is worth stating early.

## What a consultant must confirm, not the assistant

- The current construction stage of any specific block
- Any target or committed handover date
- The snagging window and defect liability period applicable to a specific booking
- Whether a specific unit is at shell-and-core or finished specification
