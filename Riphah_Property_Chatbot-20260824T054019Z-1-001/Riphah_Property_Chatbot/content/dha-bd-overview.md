## What the project is

DHA Business District is a commercial development inside a DHA-administered
sector, planned as offices, retail frontage and serviced commercial plots. It is
positioned for businesses that want an address inside a DHA sector, where the
authority handles security, utilities and estate management directly.

## Unit types

### Corporate offices

Full and part floor-plates in the tower blocks, sold shell-and-core with core
services provided to the floor. Suited to firms of ten to a hundred staff.

### Retail frontage

Ground and first-floor units on the primary boulevard. Aimed at food and
beverage, banking, and branded retail.

### Commercial plots

Serviced plots for buyers who intend to build to their own specification, within
the sector's approved building envelope, height limits and elevation controls.

## Why buyers choose a DHA sector

Three reasons come up repeatedly in enquiries:

1. **Estate management is handled.** Security, road maintenance, street lighting
   and utility coordination are DHA's responsibility, not the individual owner's.
2. **Transfer procedure is standardised.** DHA operates its own transfer office
   with a documented process, which shortens due diligence for both parties.
3. **Building controls hold value.** Elevation and height controls are enforced
   at the sector level, so a neighbouring plot cannot be developed in a way that
   undermines an adjacent building.

## Ownership and transfer

Commercial units and plots follow the DHA transfer procedure. The buyer's name is
recorded with the DHA transfer office, and the process requires the authority's
own no-objection step before a transfer completes. Timelines for that step are
set by DHA, not by the developer, and vary with the authority's own workload.

## Suitability notes for enquiries

- Buyers wanting **immediate rental income** should look at completed retail
  frontage rather than plots, which produce no yield until built.
- Buyers with a **build-to-suit requirement** — a specific floor layout, a
  showroom frontage, particular power loads — are better served by a plot than by
  a shell-and-core office floor.
- Buyers primarily seeking **capital growth** should be told plainly that the
  assistant does not forecast appreciation. Historic sector performance is a
  question for the sales team with the data in front of them.
