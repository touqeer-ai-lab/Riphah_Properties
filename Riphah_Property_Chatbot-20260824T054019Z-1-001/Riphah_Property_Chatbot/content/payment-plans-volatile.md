# Indicative Payment Plans and Price Bands

> **Placeholder document, classified `volatile`.** Every number below is invented
> for testing. It is not Riphah pricing and must not be presented as such.

## Why this document is classified volatile

Prices, availability and inventory change faster than a knowledge base can track,
and a figure stated by the assistant can be treated by a buyer as a representation
of the company (scope document s8). So this document is withheld from the model's
context entirely while the portal's `pricing_mode` is `refer` — which is the
default and the current setting.

It is present in the corpus for two reasons:

1. It proves the withholding path works. Ask the assistant about prices and it
   must refer to a consultant even though these passages exist in the database.
2. It becomes usable the day Riphah picks the `indicative` mode, without a
   re-ingest.

## Standing caveats that must accompany any figure

- Bands are indicative, per unit type, and are superseded by the price list
  current on the date of booking.
- A specific unit's price is confirmed only in writing by the sales office.
- Availability is not implied by presence in this table.
- Payment schedules are per block and per release phase; the structures above are
  typical, not universal.
