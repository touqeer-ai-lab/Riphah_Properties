# Frequently Asked Questions

> **Placeholder document.** Illustrative answers only; not Riphah-supplied policy.

## About the projects

**What is Riphah Medical City?**
A mixed-use medical district planned around a teaching hospital, comprising
medical suites, ground-floor commercial units, offices and a residential
component. It is aimed at clinicians who want to own their practice premises and
at investors seeking medical-sector tenants.

**What is DHA Business District?**
A commercial development inside a DHA-administered sector: corporate offices,
retail frontage on the primary boulevard, and serviced commercial plots.

**Can I see the site?**
Yes. Site visits are arranged through a sales consultant. Overseas buyers are
offered a scheduled video walkthrough instead.

## Buying

**Do I have to pay in full?**
No. Full payment, instalment plans, construction-linked payments and bank
financing are all available, subject to what is offered on the specific block.

**Are instalments monthly or quarterly?**
Instalment plans are typically quarterly. The exact schedule is set per block and
per release phase.

**Can I buy on bank finance?**
Yes, through banks that have approved the development. The buyer arranges the
facility and the bank makes the credit decision; the developer supplies the
documentation the bank asks for.

**Can I sell before I complete my payments?**
Yes, under the development's resale procedure. The account must be current, a
transfer fee applies, and the developer's recorded consent is needed before the
new buyer is entered.

**What happens if I cancel?**
Cancellation follows the deduction schedule in the booking documentation, which
varies by how far the payments have progressed. Ask a consultant to walk you
through the specific clause — the assistant will not characterise it.

## Overseas buyers

**I live abroad. Can I buy?**
Yes. Overseas Pakistanis buy in both projects. Payments must come through banking
channels, and many overseas buyers appoint a local nominee under an attested
power of attorney to sign and take possession.

**Which documents do I need from abroad?**
NICOP or POC, passport copy, the local nominee's CNIC if appointing one, and proof
of the remittance channel.

**Can I complete the whole purchase remotely?**
In practice, yes, with a properly attested power of attorney. The attestation
requirements depend on the country you are in.

## Medical suites specifically

**Are suites delivered fitted out?**
No. Suites are sold shell-and-core. Fit-out is the owner's responsibility and must
follow the development's medical fit-out guidelines, which cover waste handling,
plumbing loads and fire compartmentation.

**Can I run a day-care procedure room?**
Larger suites are configured for it, subject to the fit-out guidelines and the
relevant regulatory approvals for the procedures involved. Discuss the specific
use with a consultant early — it affects which suite is suitable.

**Can I rent my suite out instead of practising from it?**
Yes. Leasing to another practitioner is common, and the medical tenant profile is
the main reason investors look at this inventory.

## Charges and taxes

**What do I pay beyond the unit price?**
Transfer and registration charges set by the authority, utility connection and
meter charges, documentation and processing charges, recurring maintenance and
common-area charges from possession onward, and applicable taxes and levies.

**What are the tax implications for me?**
That depends on your own tax position, and the assistant does not give tax
advice. Speak to your tax adviser and to the sales office about the charges the
development itself levies.

## Things the assistant will not answer

**What will this be worth in five years?**
The assistant does not forecast capital appreciation or rental yield. Historic
performance is a conversation for a consultant with the data in front of them.

**Is unit 304 still available?**
Availability of a named unit is confirmed by the sales office, not by the
assistant.

**What is the exact price of a two-bed apartment in Block B?**
Pricing for a specific unit is confirmed in writing by the sales office.
