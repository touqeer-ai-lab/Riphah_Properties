# Buying Process, Payment Structure and Documentation

> **Placeholder document.** Process shape is illustrative; not Riphah-supplied
> policy. Replace with the approved sales process document before launch.

This document describes *how* a purchase works. It deliberately contains no
amounts — figures live in the payment-plan document, which is classified volatile
and is withheld from the assistant unless the portal is configured to quote
indicative ranges.

## Steps from enquiry to handover

1. **Enquiry and requirement discussion.** A consultant establishes the intended
   use, the unit type, the size band and the payment approach. Nothing is reserved
   at this stage.
2. **Site visit or video walkthrough.** Overseas buyers are offered a scheduled
   video walkthrough with a consultant on site.
3. **Unit selection and provisional hold.** A specific unit is held for a short
   window while the buyer confirms. A hold is not a booking and carries no
   contractual weight.
4. **Booking.** Booking documentation is completed and the booking amount is paid.
   The buyer receives an allotment letter identifying the unit.
5. **Instalment schedule.** Payments follow the agreed schedule. Receipts are
   issued per instalment and the running balance is available from accounts on
   request.
6. **Completion and possession.** Remaining balance, transfer charges and utility
   connection charges are settled, then possession is handed over.
7. **Title transfer.** Registration follows possession, subject to the authority's
   own procedure and timelines.

## Payment approaches available

- **Full payment** — the whole consideration at booking. Attracts the
  development's best commercial terms.
- **Instalment plans** — booking amount, then instalments over an agreed term,
  typically quarterly. Longer terms carry a higher total consideration than
  shorter ones.
- **Construction-linked payments** — instalments tied to construction milestones
  rather than to the calendar, where the block is offered on that basis.
- **Bank financing** — available through banks that have approved the
  development. The buyer arranges the facility; the developer provides the
  documentation the bank requires. Approval is the bank's decision.

## Documents a buyer needs

For a Pakistani national:

- CNIC copy (buyer, and joint buyer if applicable)
- Two passport-size photographs
- Next-of-kin CNIC copy
- Completed booking application form

For an overseas Pakistani:

- NICOP or POC copy, and passport copy
- Local nominee's CNIC where a nominee is being appointed
- Proof of remittance channel for payments from abroad

For a company purchase:

- Certificate of incorporation and memorandum
- Board resolution authorising the purchase
- CNIC of the authorised signatory

## Charges that sit outside the unit consideration

Buyers routinely miss these, and a consultant should raise them early:

- Transfer and registration charges, set by the relevant authority
- Utility connection and meter charges
- Documentation and processing charges
- Maintenance and common-area charges, which begin at possession and recur
- Applicable taxes and levies, which are the buyer's liability and change with
  federal and provincial budgets

## Overseas buyers

Payments from abroad must come through banking channels, and the remittance
record matters for the buyer's own tax position later. Overseas buyers commonly
appoint a local nominee under a registered power of attorney to sign on their
behalf and take possession; the development accepts this subject to the POA being
properly attested.

## Cancellation and refund

Cancellation before possession follows the schedule set out in the booking
documentation, which specifies the deduction applicable at each stage. A
consultant must not characterise the deduction verbally — the buyer should be
shown the clause.

## Resale before completion

An allotment may be transferred before completion under the development's resale
procedure. It requires the account to be current, carries a transfer fee, and
needs the developer's recorded consent before the new buyer's name is entered.
